# Personas

## Linh, the capsule-wardrobe professional

- 29, works in an office, owns few clothes on purpose.
- **Wants:** one tee that looks clean under a blazer and survives weekly washes.
- **Frustration:** "premium" basics that pill or warp after a month.
- **Needs from us:** fabric weight, fit, and care details stated plainly.

## Jordan, the student on a budget

- 19, first-year student, buys on a phone.
- **Wants:** a good tee at a fair price, fast.
- **Frustration:** long checkouts and surprise shipping costs.
- **Needs from us:** price and shipping shown up front, a short mobile checkout.

## Sam, the restocker

- 41, wears white tees every day and replaces them in bulk.
- **Wants:** to reorder the same size and quantity with no fuss.
- **Frustration:** brands changing the cut between batches.
- **Needs from us:** consistent sizing, easy quantity selection, multi-pack value.
