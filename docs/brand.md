# Brand

## Archetypes

| Role | Archetype | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **Innocent** | A white tee is the simplest garment there is. Innocent wants simplicity, honesty and trust, which matches one product sold with plain, true claims. |
| **Secondary** | **Everyperson** | A white tee is worn by everyone. Everyperson adds belonging and a fair price, with no status or expertise needed to buy. |

**Avoid:** luxury (Ruler) and edgy (Outlaw) cues. Belonging must never become pressure to conform.

## Voice

Warm, honest, plain. A helpful friend, not a salesperson.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Heavyweight cotton. Won't go see-through." | "The ultimate luxury tee experience." |
| "Ships in 2–4 days." | "Hurry! Selling fast!" |
| "Not sure of your size? Here's the chart." | "Perfect for everyone!" |

## Tone by context

- **Home / product:** calm, confident, short sentences.
- **Size help / errors:** reassuring, specific, no blame ("That size is out of stock. Want an email when it's back?").
- **Checkout:** minimal, factual.

## Color (60-30-10)

| Token | Hex | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#FAF8F3` | Cream background | 60% |
| `--ink` | `#1C1C1A` | Text, borders | 30% |
| `--muted` | `#6B675F` | Secondary text | (in 30%) |
| `--accent` | `#2F6B4F` | Primary CTA, few highlights | 10% |

Status colors are plain semantic (green success, red error), never the accent.

## Type

- **Headlines:** [Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces), a soft serif: warm and human (Innocent) without being childish.
- **Body & UI:** [Nunito](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito), rounded and very readable (Everyperson).
- UI text in Title Case.
