# Vision

**For** people who want one reliable white t-shirt,
**who** are tired of tees that go see-through, shrink, or yellow,
**specdriv** is an online store selling a single white t-shirt
**that** is easy to choose, easy to buy, and honest about what it is.
**Unlike** fast-fashion basics sold in a sea of options,
**we** sell one tee, described plainly, with no pressure tactics.

## Goals (MVP)

1. A visitor can go from home page to paid order in under 2 minutes.
2. Every claim on the site is true and checkable.
3. Sizing is clear enough that returns for fit stay rare.

## Out of scope (MVP)

Accounts, other colors/products, subscriptions, reviews system, discounts.
